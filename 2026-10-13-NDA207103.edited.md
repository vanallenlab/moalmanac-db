Adds the two new indications from FDA's 2026-06-24 Ibrance (palbociclib, NDA207103) label, with their derived statements which were three and 16, respectively. The evergreen Ibrance document and its label URL now point to the 2026-06-24 label. The two existing Ibrance indications were unchanged in the newer label.

## Database content updates

### Added entries

- (FDA) [HER2-negative, Hormone receptor positive, and PIK3CA activating variants and therapeutic response sensitivity to palbociclib in combination with fulvestrant and inavolisib for patients with endocrine-resistant, locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer following recurrence on or after completing adjuvant endocrine therapy](https://moalmanac.org/indications/ind:fda:ibrance:2).
- (FDA) [HER2-positive and Hormone receptor positive status and therapeutic response sensitivity to palbociclib in combination with endocrine therapy and trastuzumab, with or without pertuzumab for patients with locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer following induction treatment.](https://moalmanac.org/indications/ind:fda:ibrance:3). Statements were derived for anastrozole, exemstane, fulvestrant, and letrozole as endocrine therapies, per the agents used as endorcrine therapy in the supporting clinical trial (PATINA, NCT02947685).

### Revised entries

- [`doc:fda:ibrance`](https://moalmanac.org/documents/doc:fda:ibrance) and `url:fda:ibrance:label` updated to the 2026-06-24 label.
